THE ART OF A GOOD GUESS

Draw. Guess. Repeat.

Join with 2–5 players. The host chooses a theme, then everyone draws once in a random order. Every turn uses a different word.

Before drawing, the drawer gets 10 seconds to pick one of three random unused words. Only the drawer sees the choices. At zero, a word is picked automatically.

Choosing starts a full 80-second drawing turn. A hint letter appears with 60, 40, and 20 seconds left. Guess in chat: 100, 80, 50, or 45 points, in order.

The drawer earns 20 points per unsuccessful guesser. Correct guesses stay hidden; successful guessers sit out chat until the next turn. Spelling and spaces matter. Capitalization doesn’t.

If a player leaves midgame or loses their main or drawing connection, the game freezes, including word-picking time. Leaving reserves your seat: use Rejoin your paused game on the menu to return. It resumes once both connections return, or the host can skip disconnected players. If the drawer is skipped, their turn ends without a drawing bonus. Highest total score wins; ties share a place.
